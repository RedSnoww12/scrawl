A whiteboard that looks hand-drawn

Sketch boxes, arrows and notes that look like they were drawn on a napkin, keep as many boards as you like, and work on them live with friends.

Open the app View the code

Free and open source. Try it as a guest, no account needed.

1 Draw

  • Rectangles, diamonds, ellipses, arrows, freehand ink, text and images, all sketchy.
  • Arrows snap to shapes and stay attached when you move them.
  • Export PNG or SVG, or copy the image to the clipboard.

2 Organise

  • As many boards as you like, sorted into folders and searchable by name.
  • Sign in with Google and your boards follow you to every device.
  • Or stay a guest: boards live in your browser until you copy them over.

3 Draw together

  • Invite people as viewers or editors, or share a link anyone can open.
  • See everyone's cursor and strokes live, and follow what they see.
  • Edits made at the same time merge shape by shape, so nothing is lost.
